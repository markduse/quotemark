# 1. FAMILY SOLUTION WL (ages 0–49) — guide 9647(2/25)

Plans: **Immediate Death Benefit** (form 9772) and **Return of Premium (ROP) Death Benefit** (form 9471). ROP pays return of premium +10% interest if death in first 3 years; 100% after graded period; 100% accidental death all years (guide p5).

## Plan routing / knockout structure (guide p4 and p7) — verbatim
- Immediate Death Benefit: "Answer 'No' to all health questions 1 through 9 on the application."
- Return of Premium Death Benefit: "Answer 'No' to questions 1 through 6, 'Yes' to any health questions 7 through 9."
- "If health questions 1 through 6 are answered 'Yes', the proposed insured is not eligible for any Family Solution plans."
- p7 restates: "If any answer to questions 1 through 6 is answered 'Yes', the proposed insured is not eligible for any coverage. If any answer to questions 7 through 9 is answered 'Yes', the proposed insured is eligible for the Return of Premium Death Benefit Plan. If all questions 1 through 9 are answered 'No', the proposed insured is eligible for the Immediate Death Benefit Plan."
- The guide does NOT reprint the application questions 1–9 verbatim (app Form 9617 / ICC15-GL212). The Medical Impairment Guide maps each condition to its question number (noted below as Q#).

## Tobacco (guide p6) — verbatim question
"During the past 12 months have you used tobacco in any form?" — "Tobacco in any form includes cigarettes, electronic cigarettes (e-cigs), chewing tobacco, cigars, pipes, snuff, nicotine patch, nicotine gum/aerosol/inhaler, Hookah pipe, clove, or bidis cigarettes." (No occasional-cigar carve-out on this product.) Tobacco affects rate class only, except the diabetes combo rule below.

## A) COPD / Emphysema / Chronic Bronchitis

Medical Impairment Guide (pp 31–33):
| Condition | Criteria (verbatim window) | Outcome | Q# | Page |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chronic Bronchitis | "Medically diagnosed, treated, or taken medication within the past three years" | Return of Premium | 9 | 31 |
| COPD | "Medically diagnosed, treated, or taken medication within the past three years" | Return of Premium | 9 | 31 |
| Emphysema | "Medically diagnosed, treated, or taken medication within the past three years" | Return of Premium | 9 | 32 |

Implication: COPD/emphysema/chronic bronchitis treated/medicated **within 3 years → ROP plan**; beyond 3 years → does not trigger (Immediate possible). Never an outright decline by itself on this product.

Prescription Reference Guide (pp 16–30) — inhalers/respiratory meds. Rule header: if med filled within the 'RX FILL WITHIN' window, the listed outcome applies; 'N/A' = timing irrelevant.
- **Advair, Albuterol, Atrovent/Atrovent HFA/Atrovent (Nasal), Azmacort, Combivent, Daliresp, Dilor, Duoneb, Ipratropium Bromide, Proventil, Serevent, Spiriva, Symbicort, Theo-Dur, Theophylline, Ventolin, Xopenex** — use = COPD/Chronic Bronchitis/Emphysema: RX fill within **Three years → Return of Premium** (pp 16–18, 19–20, 26–30).
- Same meds when use = **Asthma** (Advair, Albuterol, Azmacort, Dilor, Proventil, Serevent, Symbicort, Theo-Dur, Theophylline, Ventolin, Xopenex) → **Immediate** (N/A timing).
- Atrovent / Ipratropium Bromide when use = Allergies → **Immediate**.
- Combivent, Daliresp, Duoneb, Spiriva have no asthma alternative — COPD-only meds, 3-year window → ROP.

Tobacco interaction: no COPD+tobacco combo rule on Family Solution (tobacco combo exists only for diabetes: "If taking insulin shots or using tobacco within the past 12 months, the insured will not be eligible for coverage" — # footnote, every Rx page).

If controlled with three or more medications, the insured will not be eligible for coverage." (= no coverage), while the impairment guide p32 says 3+ meds → ROP. Conservative read for quoting: ≤2 BP meds = OK for Immediate; 3+ BP meds = at best ROP, possibly decline.

Prescription Reference Guide heart rules (pp 16–30):
- **Any HTN med when the use is CHF** → No Coverage (N/A). Applies to the entire HTN list (Accupril, Aceon, Aldactone, Altace, Atenolol, Benicar, Bystolic, Capoten, Carvedilol, Coreg, Cozaar, Diovan, Furosemide, Lasix, Lisinopril, Losartan, Metoprolol, Micardis, Nadolol, Norvasc-class, Toprol XL, etc.).
- **Angina / CHF nitrates & anti-anginals → No Coverage (N/A):** Amyl Nitrate (p16), Dilatrate SR (p20), Imdur (p22), Isordil, Isosorbide Dinitrate/Mononitrate (p22), Minitran (p24), Monoket (p24), Nitrek, Nitro-bid, Nitro-dur, Nitroglycerine/Nitrostat/Nitroquick, Nitrol (p25), Ranexa (p26), Vascor (Angina) (p29).
- **CHF-specific meds → No Coverage (N/A):** BiDil (p18), Eplerenone (p21), Inamrinone, Inspra (p22), Lotensin (CHF use) (p23), Milrinone (CHF/Cardiomyopathy) (p24), Natrecor (p25), Primacor (CHF/Cardiomyopathy) (p26).
- **Irregular-heartbeat meds — RX fill within Three years → Return of Premium:** Betapace (p18), Cordarone (p19), Digitek, Digoxin (p20), Lanoxicaps, Lanoxin (p23), Norpace (p25), Pacerone (p25), Rythmol (p27), Tambocor (p28). (Same meds' CHF use → No Coverage.)
- **Blood-clot meds — RX fill within Three years → Return of Premium:** Aggrenox, Coumadin (p19), Heparin (p22), Warfarin (p29). BUT when the use is "Stroke / Heart or Circulatory Disease or Disorder" (Warfarin also "/ Heart Valve Disease") → **No Coverage** (N/A).
- **Stroke/circulatory meds → No Coverage (N/A):** Clopidogrel (p19), Plavix (p26), Nimodipine, Nimotop (p25).

Net logic: any true heart disease (MI, CAD, angina, CHF, cardiomyopathy, valve disease, stroke, circulatory disorder) = **No Coverage, ever** (Q6a). Rhythm problems (afib/irregular heartbeat), aneurysm, and blood clots = **3-year lookback → ROP**, older than 3 years → can be Immediate. Pacemaker, stent, bypass are not named on Family Solution's impairment list, but stent/bypass fall under Heart Disease/Disorder (Q6a) = No Coverage.

## C) Cancer

Medical Impairment Guide (p31): **Cancer** — "Medically diagnosed, treated, or taken medication for internal cancer, lymphoma, melanoma or Hodgkin's Disease or history of metastatic cancer **within the past five years**" → **No Coverage** (Q4). Beyond 5 years → not triggered (Immediate possible).
- **Leukemia** — diagnosed/treated/medicated ever → **No Coverage** (Q6a, p32).
- Basal/squamous cell skin cancer: not listed as a knockout (only "internal cancer, lymphoma, melanoma or Hodgkin's" named) — treat as not a trigger; melanoma IS a trigger.

Prescription Reference Guide cancer meds — verbatim: "Five years → No Coverage; > Five years → Immediate": Arimidex (p17), Campath (p18), Casodex (p19), Cytoxan (p20), Femara (p21), Gleevec (p21), Hydroxyurea (p22), Intron-A (Cancer use; Hepatitis C use → ROP) (p22), Leukeran (p23), Lupron (p24), Methotrexate (Cancer 5y; Rheumatoid Arthritis use → ROP) (p24), Proleukin (p26), Rituxan (Cancer 5y; RA use → ROP) (p27), Sprycel (p27), Tamoxifen (p28), Valstar (p29), Xeloda (p30).

## Other Family Solution notes relevant to quoting
- Build chart p11 decides Immediate vs ROP vs decline by weight (unisex; e.g. 5'8": Immediate max 289, ROP 290–316, above 316 decline; minimums also apply).
- Max face: Immediate $35,000 (0–49); ROP $20,000 (18–49); min $5,000; $30 policy fee (p4). (Products at a Glance 8/25 shows min face $10,000 — newer; guide says $5,000.)
- ROP plan cannot carry ADB, Waiver of Premium, Children's rider, or Confined Care rider (p4, p15).

# 3. EASY TERM (10/20/30-yr term to 95; 20/30-yr 75% ROP) — guide 3341(10/20)

Forms 06-9690 (AA, OL, PA, PS) / LTL101 (IAA); ROP form 18-3483. App Form 3488. Issue ages (nearest): 10-yr 18–70, 20-yr 18–65, 30-yr 18–55, 20-yr ROP 18–60, 30-yr ROP 18–50 (p4).
