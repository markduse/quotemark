## Discrepancies & flags for the engine

1. **American Amicable tier order conflict.** DOC 1 (pp. 2-3, 5, 8) and DOC 2 say heart events/cancer are "Within 2 yrs - **ROP**; Within 3 yrs - **Graded**", but DOC 3 reverses it: "2 years **Graded**, 3 years **Return of Premium**, > 3 years Immediate". The two grid sheets agree with each other; DOC 3 is the outlier — prefer ROP@2 / Graded@3, but verify with the carrier guide before hard-coding.
2. **Aetna Accendo melanoma conflict.** DOC 1 p. 6: "Basal cell and Squamous - Preferred". DOC 2 p. 6: "Within 3 years - Modified (but excludes basal / squamous cell skin cancer)". Both agree basal/squamous = Preferred; DOC 2 adds a 3-yr Modified window for true melanoma.
3. **Transamerica CHF conflict.** DOC 1 p. 3: "Prior to age 45 - Decline; Onset age 45-80 - Graded"; DOC 3: "Pref. 2 Yr 1st Diagnosed" — irreconcilable; DOC 1 is the more conservative, structured source.
4. **Americo "Guaranteed Issue" outcomes** mean the case is placed on **Eagle Guaranteed** (GI, 3-yr graded ROP death benefit), not declined; ">1 yr"-type entries on DOC 3 mean Eagle **Premier** (level SI) becomes available after the window.
5. **Oxygen is the COPD kill switch** at most carriers (MOO/Prosperity/AmAm = Decline; Aetna/Foresters = Decline within 1 yr; Aetna explicitly "CPAP OK") — but **Transamerica takes oxygen users at Graded** (DOC 1 p. 7; DOC 3 "Graded if O2") and **Americo Guaranteed takes O2 within 6 months**.
6. **Tobacco x COPD** only appears in DOC 3 (CFG): smoker with COPD drops from Select to Advantage (graded) even without oxygen. No other carrier in these docs ties tobacco to a condition outcome (Americo's "Quit Smoking Advantage", DOC 1 p. 10 / DOC 2 p. 9, is a rate-class perk only).
7. **"Not asked - allowed"** rows (AFib at Americo/Prosperity/Aetna/AmAm/Foresters; TIA at Americo) are underwriting silence, not approval of severity — meds may still be caught by Rx screens ("check med list" rows).
8. DOC 2 lacks Cardiomyopathy and Transamerica entirely; DOC 1 is the superset for those six carriers plus Trans; DOC 3 uniquely adds AIG, Americo UP, CFG, RNA.
