## ⚠️ CRITICAL SCOPE FINDING (read first)

**This guide does NOT print the Section A or Section B health questions.** It is a 3-page agent process guide: it defines the tier-decision logic, issue ages/faces, required forms, and administrative decline rules — and nothing else. Specifically, the document contains:

- **NO Section A question text** (knockout questions) — not printed anywhere.
- **NO Section B question text** (graded questions) — not printed anywhere.
- **NO condition-specific rules of any kind** — zero mentions of COPD, emphysema, bronchitis, oxygen, inhalers, heart attack/MI, stent, bypass, CHF, cardiomyopathy, angina, CAD, atrial fibrillation, pacemaker, valve, aneurysm, stroke, TIA, cancer, melanoma, or any other named condition. No lookback windows (12/24/36-month, etc.) appear anywhere in the PDF.
- **NO Rx/medication list** and **NO build (height/weight) chart**.

The health questions live on **the application itself** ("Our Yes/No application", p.2; "All medical questions on the application have been answered", p.3; "The physician/hospital section on page 3 [of the application] is required", p.3). **To get Section A/B verbatim for the quoting app, the Survivor application (or the carrier's Survivor Quote App logic) is required — this PDF cannot supply them.** LifeShield also publishes a "Survivor Quote App calculator" for smartphones (p.2) that encodes the correct premiums.

## 1) Tier-decision logic (p.2 — verbatim, the only underwriting rules in the document)

From "Field Underwriting Has Never Been Easier…" bullets, quoted exactly:

> - "All 'No' answers to Section A and Sections B will result in a Level Benefit plan of either tobacco or non-tobacco."
> - "Any 'Yes' response to questions in Sections A is not an acceptable risk and should not be submitted."
> - "Any 'Yes' response in Sections B will result in a Graded Death Benefit plan with no change in premium rate (or commission)."

**App logic:**
| Answers | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Any Section A = Yes | **DECLINE** (do not submit) |
| All A = No, any Section B = Yes | **Graded Death Benefit** — same premium rate as Level (no rate change, no commission change) |
| All A = No, all B = No | **Level Benefit**, Tobacco or Non-Tobacco class |

- Rate classes: **Tobacco / Non-Tobacco only** (no preferred tiers). Graded uses the same premium rate as Level.
- Decision is knowable at point of sale: "know the product your client qualifies for when you leave the sales interview" (p.2).

## 2) Issue ages, face amounts, product structure (p.2)

- **Issue Ages: 45–80** (verbatim "Issue Ages 45-80").
- **Minimum Death Benefit $5,000** / **Maximum Death Benefit $30,000** (verbatim).
- Benefit options: **Lump Sum or Income Replacement** benefit options (p.2).
- **Joint First to Die** applications available — "can save your clients up to 100% when compared to the competition" (p.2).
- Joint rating rule (p.3, verbatim): "For Joint applications, the premium rate is based on the age of the oldest applicant and whether either uses tobacco (including marijuana, vapes, or e-cigs)."
  - Note for app: LifeShield's tobacco definition **includes marijuana, vapes, and e-cigarettes**.
- Individual Survivor Plan: "A separate application is required for each individual if applying for the Individual Survivor Plan" (p.3).
- Graded death benefit schedule: **NOT specified in this guide** (the graded payout schedule — e.g., ROP+% years 1–2 — is not printed here).

## 3) Sales/eligibility process rules (p.2)

- **In-person only:** "You must meet all applicants in person when taking the application. (No Phone or Virtual Sales Permitted)".
- Photo-ID verification required (driver's license, passport, or other government-issued ID with name, DOB, photograph).
- Agent must ask each question, list **all doctors and hospitals as requested on the application** (p.3 checklist: "list all doctors and hospitals visited within the last 24 months").

## 4) Home Office processing / Rx check (p.2)

Verbatim: "Home Office Processing… may include an inquiry to MIB, Inc or a Prescription Drug Report from Milliman IntelliScript. If the application is incomplete or inconsistent with MIB or Milliman, a phone interview will be conducted with the applicant. Otherwise, a Medical Information Verification form will be sent to the applicant for confirmation."

- So: **MIB + Milliman IntelliScript Rx sweep** are possible, but **no medication list / Rx rules are published** in this guide. Inconsistency triggers a phone interview, not an automatic decline.

## 5) Automatic Declines — non-health (p.3, verbatim list)

"Automatic Declines… reasons for declines other than poor health:"
- "If application is taken by phone or virtually"
- "The applicant's personal information is not complete and has not been furnished upon request."
- "The application was not received in the home office within 10 days of the application being signed."
- "The applicant does not recall filling out or denies signing the application."
- "The applicant is unable or unwilling to complete a phone interview if required."

## 6) Required forms & payment rules (p.2–3)

Required before processing (p.2): Application; Sample Accelerated Death Benefit Calculation (⇒ product carries an **Accelerated Death Benefit rider**); Replacement form if replacing insurance; Voided check + Bank Draft form if paying by monthly bank draft; Check for first month's premium if not bank draft (check cannot be postdated).

Payment/admin (p.3): **"Direct Monthly is not an available payment option."** Bank-draft date must be between the **1st and 28th**. Pre-Authorization EFT form invalid if any info missing or if modal-premium discrepancy exceeds **$1.00**. Effective date = approval date (check) or draft date (bank draft); requested effective date cannot be more than **45 days** after application signing. Save-age allowed via CWA (back premium + initial premium) or immediate 1st-premium bank authorization. Applicants born outside the U.S.: proof of legal residency required (U.S. Passport, citizenship papers, or Green Card).

## 7) Contact

Underwriting Department: **1-833-989-0036** (p.3).
