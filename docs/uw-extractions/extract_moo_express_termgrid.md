# PART 1 — MUTUAL OF OMAHA EXPRESS PRODUCTS REFERENCE (Simplified UW, 27857_0823)

Products covered: **Term Life Express (TLE)** and **IUL Express (IULE)** — one shared simplified-UW rule set for both. (Living Promise Level/Graded FE also appears on p.1 for build/ages only; no condition rules for it in this doc.)

## 1.2 Governing rule (verbatim, p.2)

> "Multiple impairments resulting in a rating greater than Table 4 will be declined for our Express products. Below are some examples of multiple impairments that would result in a decline."

And after both tables (verbatim): "**Note:** This is not a complete list. Please refer to the Life Insurance Underwriting Guidelines for additional impairments."

| Multiple impairments | Offer |

### COPD / cardiac / cancer — what this document does and does NOT say
- **COPD:** not named in this reference doc. (The 2020 term grid, Part 2, records MOO TLE COPD as "ever had - DECLINE"; the full MOO Life UW Guidelines PDF is the authoritative source.)
- **Cardiac:** the only cardiac item here is **Pacemaker = automatic Decline**. No other cardiac rules in this doc.
- **Cancer:** the only oncology item here is **Hodgkin's Disease = automatic Decline**. No general cancer window given. (2020 grid, Part 2: MOO TLE cancer = "Excluding Basal Cell or Squamous cell skin cancer - ok; Otherwise - DECLINE".)
- This doc is an examples sheet, not the complete knockout list — it twice points to the full "Life Insurance Underwriting Guidelines" (we hold `Mutual of Omaha - Life UW Guidelines (Brokerage, Jan 2021).pdf` in the same folder).

# PART 2 — MULTI-CARRIER TERM UW GRID SHEET (2020)

## ⚠️ VINTAGE WARNING — CROSS-CHECK ONLY
**This grid is 2020-dated (John Hancock Vitality on the shelf, pre-2021 product lineups).
