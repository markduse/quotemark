## A) PRODUCT STRUCTURE

### Tiers / Underwriting classes [PG p4]

Two death-benefit structures, five underwriting classes. There is **no Graded tier and no Guaranteed Issue** — the ladder is Level Preferred → Level Standard (Non-tobacco/Tobacco) → Modified → Not eligible.

| Death benefit | Underwriting classes |
|---|---|
| Level | Level Preferred, Level Non-tobacco (Standard), Level Tobacco |
| Modified | Modified Non-tobacco, Modified Tobacco |

Note: the UW outcome tables (UG pp2–6, PG pp6–10) use outcomes **Preferred / Standard / Modified / Not eligible**. "Standard" maps to the Level Non-tobacco or Level Tobacco class; tobacco status is determined separately (see Tobacco row in lifestyle table).

### Issue ages (age last birthday) [PG p4]

| Class | Issue ages |
|---|---|
| Level Non-tobacco (incl. Preferred) | 50–85 |
| Level Tobacco | 50–80 |
| Modified Non-tobacco | 50–80 |
| Modified Tobacco | 50–75 |

### Face amounts [PG p4; CG p2]

Increments of $1,000. **No face-amount pricing bands** [PG p5].

| Tier | Ages | Min | Max |
|---|---|---|---|
| Level | 50–74 | $2,000 | $35,000 |
| Level | 75+ | $2,000 | $20,000 for Preferred; $15,000 for Level Non-Tobacco and Tobacco |
| Modified | 50–74 | $2,000 | $20,000 |
| Modified | 75–80 | $2,000 | $10,000 |

### Modified death benefit schedule [PG p4]

- Year 1 = 110% of premiums paid
- Year 2 = 120% of premiums paid
- Year 3 = 100% of face amount
- If death is the result of an accident, full face amount is paid.
- (May vary by state.)

Level = full death benefit all years. [PG p4]

### State availability

- Carrier licensed in 49 states, DC, USVI (not NY). [PG footers, p16]
- Product **currently NOT available in: California, Florida, New York, South Carolina.** [PG p5]
- Consumer brochure additionally "not for use in ID, MS, NC, OR, WV" (marketing-material restriction, not stated as product unavailability). [CG p1, p3, p4]

### Riders [PG p4, p11]

1. **Accelerated Death Benefit Rider for Terminal Illness** — automatically included, no cost, both tiers. Owner may accelerate a portion of the eligible death benefit upon terminal-illness diagnosis.
2. **Accidental Death Benefit Rider** — optional, additional cost, **Level benefit product only**, elected at issue. Ages 50–80; coverage and premiums cease at age 100. Benefit $2,000–$35,000 and must match (same death benefit amount as) the base policy. Not available on Modified.

### Eligibility criteria (pre-application, all 3 required) [UG p2; PG p6]

1. Be a US citizen or permanent resident
2. Not replacing an existing Continental General policy
3. Applying and signing in their state of residence

### e-App process [PG p13–15]

- 100% digital eApp at **newbridgelife.com** (also the Producer Portal). Producer registers with NPN + Continental General producer number; credentials validated with carrier at registration and each login.
- Completed in a single sitting: face-to-face, video call, or phone. eConsent + eSignature; no medical exam, no blood tests; instant decision in most cases.
- UW data pulls: prescription medication history, health insurance claims records, prior insurance activity (MIB). [UG p2; PG p6]
- Flow: Quote inputs and quote → Eligibility criteria → Application questions → eConsent and disclosures → Beneficiary designation → Underwriting decisioning → **Offer / Not eligible / MIB pending** → Payment → Review and eSignature → Producer certification → Policy issue. [PG p13]

### The three UW families, summarized for the quoting engine

**COPD / respiratory:**
- COPD ever → Standard. Chronic bronchitis ever → Standard. Chronic emphysema ever → Standard. Chronic respiratory condition (excluding asthma or sleep apnea) ever → Standard. [UG p3–4]
- **Currently prescribed oxygen equipment** (due to ongoing illness/medical condition, same row as wheelchair/scooter/walker) → **Not eligible**. [UG p5]
- No inhaler/nebulizer rule is printed anywhere in these PDFs.
- COPD + tobacco: no printed combination rule. Tobacco in last 12 months simply rates the Tobacco class; COPD alone is Standard. (No COPD-smoker knockout appears in these documents.)

**Heart / cardiovascular:**
- Heart attack (MI): <2 yr Modified; 2–<5 yr Standard; >5 yr Preferred.
- Angina: <2 yr Modified; 2–<5 yr Standard; >5 yr Preferred.
- Angioplasty (stent) surgery: last 12 mo Not eligible; 1–<2 yr Modified; 2–<5 yr Standard; >5 yr Preferred.
- Heart bypass surgery: last 12 mo Not eligible; 1–<2 yr Modified; 2–<5 yr Standard; >5 yr Preferred.
- Heart valve surgery: same ladder as bypass.
- Pacemaker implant: same ladder as bypass.
- CHF/heart failure: **ever → Not eligible.** Cardiomyopathy: **ever → Not eligible.** Defibrillator implanted: **ever → Not eligible.** Pulmonary hypertension: ever → Not eligible.
- Stroke: <2 yr Modified; 2–<5 yr Standard; >5 yr Preferred. TIA: identical ladder.
- Atrial fibrillation: **not listed** in these PDFs. Aneurysm: **not listed.** CAD as a standalone diagnosis: not listed (only its treatments/events above). Diabetes + any heart disorder → Not eligible.

**Cancer:**
- Cancer (excluding basal/squamous cell skin cancer), lymphoma, **melanoma**, multiple myeloma, leukemia, or chemo/radiation/any cancer treatment (received or advised): last 12 months → Not eligible; 1–<2 yr → Modified; **>2 years → Standard**.
- Recurrent cancer (same or different, excluding basal/squamous cell) → ever Not eligible.
- Basal cell and squamous cell skin cancers are explicitly carved out (no rating). Melanoma is treated as cancer, NOT as skin-cancer carve-out.
- Bone marrow transplant or stem cell treatment (received or advised) → ever Not eligible.

## Quoting-app implementation notes

1. **Missing for quoting:** rate tables (must be sourced separately), commission percentages, verbatim eApp questions, Rx list.
2. Class ladder for the rules engine: Preferred > Standard (Non-tobacco or Tobacco) > Modified > Decline. Tobacco last 12 mo → Tobacco rates; 1–<2 yr → Standard; >2 yr → Preferred-eligible.
3. Unusually liberal calls worth surfacing in the UI: COPD/emphysema/chronic bronchitis ever = Standard; cancer clean >2 yr = Standard; diabetes w/ neuropathy or retinopathy = Preferred; major depression/anxiety = Preferred; Parkinson's, MS, chronic hepatitis, chronic kidney disease = Standard.
4. Hard declines to encode (ever): CHF, cardiomyopathy, defibrillator, pulmonary hypertension, dialysis, cirrhosis, dementia/Alzheimer's/cognitive impairment, HIV/AIDS, ALS, Huntington's, muscular dystrophy, cystic fibrosis, schizophrenia, organ/bone-marrow/stem-cell transplant (incl. advised), recurrent cancer, chronic pancreatitis, diabetes+amputation/circulatory/coma/heart/insulin-shock.
5. Current-status declines: oxygen, wheelchair/scooter/walker, ADL assistance, nursing home/assisted living (incl. advised), home healthcare/hospice (incl. advised), hospitalized/rehab, pending procedures/tests/results (12 mo), terminal illness ≤12 mo life expectancy.
6. State gate: exclude CA, FL, NY, SC (as of PG 12/08/25 print).
7. Two source-PDF typos flagged inline: BMI Preferred band ("18.0–44.0") and Build Chart 6'5" Preferred max/Standard min gap.
