# Occidental Life (American-Amicable Group) — Underwriting Extraction: Home Protector + Easy Term

**Sources** (both in `~/Downloads/`, agent guides, FULL versions read cover-to-cover including complete Rx sections):
1. `Occidental Life Home Protector UW.pdf` — guide form **3343(10/20)**, CN16-111. 39 PDF pages; printed pages 1–40 (printed page = PDF page + 1 from PDF p.2 on). Medical Impairment Guide printed pp. 22–25; Prescription Reference Guide printed pp. 26–39.
2. `Occidental Life Easy Term UW (1).pdf` — guide form **3341(10/20)**, CN16-109. 43 PDF pages; printed pages 1–44 (printed page = PDF page + 1 from PDF p.2 on). Medical Impairment Guide printed pp. 26–29; Prescription Reference Guide printed pp. 30–43.

All page cites below are the **printed** page numbers shown in the guide footers.

**Shared underwriting model (verbatim structure):** Both plans are "Simplified Issue, underwritten standard through table 4. **NOT GUARANTEED ISSUE**." (HP p.4; ET p.5). "Underwriting decisions will be made on an accept/reject basis (no table ratings available). Applications on individuals who are considered above a table 4 risk, will be declined." (HP p.16; ET p.19). Automated mobile-app decision outcomes: "'Approved', 'Refer to Home Office', or 'Declined'." (HP p.15; ET p.18).

**Impairment-guide decision columns:** each condition row gives a separate outcome for **LIFE | DI RIDER (DIR) | AODIR | CRITICAL ILL RIDER (CIR)** plus a "QUESTION ON APP" cross-reference. Outcomes are only ever **Standard** or **Decline**. `*` footnote (every impairment page, both guides): "Underwriting will consider issuing DIR/AODIR with an exclusion rider. Contact Underwriting Department for details at Underwriting@aatx.com."

**About "application knockout questions":** Neither agent guide reprints the Section A/B/C health-question text. The application forms themselves (HP: Form No. **3491**, HP p.4; ET: Form No. **3488**, ET p.4) carry the questions; the guides only map each impairment to its question number (captured in the tables below as "App Q"). The only health question printed verbatim in either guide is the tobacco question (captured below). Standing knockout instruction, verbatim: "If the Proposed Insured has a condition which is listed in the **Medical Impairment Guide** as a **Decline** or if he or she exceeds either the maximum or minimum weight in the **Build Chart** provided in this guide, the application should not be submitted to the Home Office." (HP p.17; ET p.21, repeated p.21 bottom.)

# PART 1 — HOME PROTECTOR (Policy Forms 3274 / 3482 ROP)

## 1.1 Product snapshot
- Simplified issue term to age 95; 15/20/25/30-yr level premium; ROP on 20/25/30 (HP p.4).
- Issue ages (age **last** birthday): 15-yr 20–65; 20-yr 20–60; 25-yr 20–55; 30-yr 20–50; ROP same as base term (HP p.4).
- Face $25,000–$300,000 (min $25k face or $25.00/mo premium, whichever greater); **Unisex** rates; Tobacco/Non-Tobacco; policy fee $80; modal factors .088/.262/.519 (HP p.4).
- **Mortgage Requirement** — "a current mortgage is required regardless of the date originally taken or refinanced" (HP p.4).
- Eligibility basis: "a simplified application, liberal height and weight chart, a check with the Medical Information Bureau (MIB) and pharmaceutical related facility, and a telephone interview (if applicable)." (HP p.16).
- Non-med limits: $25,000–149,999 → no interview any age; $150,000–300,000 ages 56–65 → **T = Telephone Interview** (ages 20–55 none) (HP p.19). MVR ordered when applying for ADB (HP p.19).
- Build chart HP p.20 (min weight → decline below; above Table-4 max → decline; **"If the applicant has a medical condition combined with build that exceeds table 2, the applicant is not eligible for coverage."** HP p.20).
- Re-apply rule: no new application if 2 policies with any of the group's companies lapsed/NTO/surrendered/cancelled in previous 12 months, or 3+ in past 5 years (HP p.17). Third-Party Payor: "we DO NOT accept Home Protector applications where a Third Party Payor is involved." (HP p.18).

## 1.2 Tobacco question (verbatim — only app question printed in guide)
"**During the past 12 months have you used tobacco in any form?** Check '**Yes**' or '**No**' — This includes the use of cigarettes, electronic cigarettes (e-cigs), chewing tobacco, cigars, pipes, snuff, nicotine patch, nicotine gum/aerosol/inhaler, Hookah pipe, clove or bidis cigarettes. Excludes occasional cigar or pipe use." (HP p.16)
