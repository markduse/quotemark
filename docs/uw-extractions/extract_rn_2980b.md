## CRITICAL FINDING FOR THE QUOTING APP

**This guide contains NO impairment-specific underwriting tables.** After reading all 24 pages:

- There is **no COPD / emphysema / chronic bronchitis section** (no oxygen rule, no inhaler rule, no COPD+tobacco rule).
- There is **no cardiovascular condition table** (no MI/stent/bypass/CHF/cardiomyopathy/angina/CAD/afib/pacemaker/defibrillator/valve/aneurysm/stroke/TIA lookback windows).
- There is **no cancer table** (no remission windows, no basal/squamous/melanoma carve-outs).
- There is **no medication / Rx knockout list.** Page 6 states verbatim: **"There is no knock-out medication list for Jet Term Life and Jet Whole Life."**
- There are **no printed application knockout questions** anywhere in the guide.

Instead, Jet Term/Jet WL use an **algorithmic Accelerated UW process** (p6): "The rules behind the application are set to evaluate all health conditions and history provided by the applicant. The system also matches medications, driving record, and MIB responses to answers on the application." Health-condition outcomes are decided by the engine / underwriter, not by a published field grid.

**Implication:** Any RN condition rules currently in the quoting app for COPD, cardiac, or cancer did NOT come from 2980-B. RN final-expense (SIWL/GDB) impairment rules must be sourced from **Agent Guide 2996-B** (likely the companion file `Royal Neighbors - Whole Life UW Guidelines.pdf` in the same folder — not read as part of this task). Do not map 2980-B products to FE cheat-sheet assumptions.

Note for the app's product model: **Jet Whole Life here is NOT the SIWL final expense product.** It is an accelerated/fully-underwritten WL ($25,000–$5,000,000 bands, substandard through Table 16). The FE-style SIWL lives in 2996-B.

## A) COPD / Emphysema / Chronic Bronchitis

**No rules printed in this guide** for any product (Jet Term, Jet WL, SPWL). No oxygen, inhaler, nebulizer, or COPD-tobacco interaction language appears anywhere. Handled by the accelerated engine / underwriter referral (p6) or the SPWL phone interview (p14). For a risk pre-assessment agents are told to call Underwriting: (800) 627-4762, option 1 (p6, p21).

## B) Heart / Cardiovascular

Only two cardiovascular references exist in the entire guide:

1. **Conditional Receipt exclusion (p7, verbatim):** "If the face amount applied for is over $1 million, or if within the past 12 months the Proposed Insured has been treated for or had any known **heart trouble, stroke, or cancer**, then payment (including authorization to draft the first premium) should not be submitted with the application. Additionally, no conditional receipt will be given, and there will be no coverage under any conditional receipt."
   - This is a *conditional-receipt/temporary-coverage* rule, NOT an eligibility or class rule. It does not say the applicant is declined — only that no premium is drafted and no interim coverage exists while underwriting.
2. **Family history for Preferred classes (p12):**
   - Super Preferred: "No incidence of coronary or cardiovascular disease or cancer in either parent or sibling prior to age 60."
   - Preferred: "No death from coronary or cardiovascular disease, or cancer in either parent or siblings prior to age 60."
   - Preferred Tobacco: same as Preferred.
   - Standard (Non-Tobacco/Tobacco): N/A.

No MI, stent, bypass, CHF, cardiomyopathy, angina, CAD, afib, pacemaker, defibrillator, valve, or aneurysm rules are printed. No stroke/TIA lookback other than the 12-month conditional-receipt rule above.

## C) Cancer

Only two cancer references (same two locations as cardiac):

1. **Conditional Receipt (p7):** cancer treated/known within past 12 months → no premium collected with app, no conditional-receipt coverage (verbatim quote above).
2. **Family history for Preferred classes (p12):** cancer in parent/sibling prior to age 60 disqualifies Super Preferred (any incidence) and Preferred/Preferred Tobacco (death from).

No personal-history cancer table, no remission windows, no basal/squamous or melanoma carve-outs, no type list.

## Medication rules

- **p6 verbatim: "There is no knock-out medication list for Jet Term Life and Jet Whole Life."**
- p6: Accelerated system "matches medications, driving record, and MIB responses to answers on the application"; mismatch between stated health history and Rx/MIB/MVR results → "the app will most likely be referred to the underwriting staff."
- p11: "MIB, MVR and Rx profile ordered on all applicants" (Jet Term/Jet WL).
- p14: "MIB and Prescription History ordered on all applicants" (SPWL).

## What the guide DOES specify (useful for the quoting app)

### Jet Term Life & Jet Whole Life — UW requirements by age/amount (p11)

Preferred consideration only for fully underwritten face amounts. MIB, MVR, Rx profile on all applicants.

| Issue Age | $25,000–250,000 | $250,001–500,000 | $500,001–2,000,000 | $2,000,001–5,000,000 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 18–50 | Accelerated UW | Accelerated UW | PM, IR | PM, IR, FINC |
| 51–60 | Accelerated UW | PM | PM, IR, SB | PM, IR, SB, APS, FINC |
| 61–65 | PM | MA, SB, APS | PM, MA, EKG, APS, IR, FINC | PM, MA, EKG, APS, IR, FINC |
| 66–80 (Jet WL only) | PM | MA, SB, APS | PM, MA, EKG, APS, IR, FINC | PM, MA, EKG, APS, IR, FINC |

Key: SB = NT-proBNP blood test; APS = medical records; IR = electronic inspection report; FINC = financials; PM = paramed; MA = mature assessment (p8: "get up and go test" + ADL questions at paramed).

### Risk classes (p11)

- **Accelerated UW:** Preferred Non-Tobacco, Standard Non-Tobacco, Standard Tobacco, Substandard 1 Non-Tobacco (1 class includes up to Table 4), Substandard 1 Tobacco (up to Table 4), Substandard 2 Non-Tobacco, Substandard 2 Tobacco.
- **Traditional UW:** Super Preferred Non-Tobacco, Preferred Non-Tobacco, Preferred Tobacco, Standard Non-Tobacco, Standard Tobacco, multiple substandard rating classes up to Table 16.

### Accelerated UW referral triggers (p6)

Optimized for under age 50 in relatively good health. Referred to underwriter if: age 18–26 (e-signature authentication); "yes" to any replacement question; agent answered "no" to written-sales-material question; health history doesn't match Rx/MIB/MVR; existing RNA coverage (added to requested amount, may push case to fully underwritten and out of Accelerated).

### SPWL requirements (p14)

MIB + Rx history on all applicants. Requirements by Net Amount at Risk: up to $49,999 → telephone interview (both age bands 45–65 and 66–80); $50,000–99,999 → phone interview (+APS if 66–80); over $100,000 → phone interview + APS (both bands). Where APS required but no doctor visit in past 12 months → mature assessment exam + blood profile/UA ordered. Point-of-sale phone interview can give a POS decision where no APS needed: (800) 627-4762, Option 1, Option 1, 8am–5pm CT. Minimum premium $5,000 no exceptions; pre-authorization required over $200,000 premium. Source-of-funds docs: cash $10k–$24,999 indicate origin on app p2; $25k–$49,999 SPWL Declaration of Source of Funds; $50k+ bank statements/proof. 1035: life-to-life only (annuity-to-life not acceptable), identical owner+insured, contract in force, entire value exchanged; $10,000 minimum.

### Other knockouts / global rules (non-medical)

- Felony (insured and/or owner): not accepted regardless of face amount (p8).
- Bankruptcy must be discharged before applying; liens/judgments satisfied or payment-plan evidence (p6).
- Residents of AK, HI, AL, LA, NH, NY, MA: cannot accept (p4, p10).
- Foreign nationals, visa holders, no-SSN applicants: not eligible; must be U.S. citizen or legal resident (p8).
- Military alerted/on orders/deployed to active duty: unacceptable, decision postponed (p8).
- Level 4 "No Travel" destinations / armed-conflict travel: may not be accepted, call (p8).
- 3+ lapsed certificates: cannot consider; 2 lapsed: cover letter + new app + first premium; 1 lapsed within 12 mo: reapply with new app (p8).
- Financial limits (p7): max face = multiple of annual earned income — under 18 individual consideration; 18–40: 20x; 41–50: 15x; 51–69: 10x; over 69: 5x. Premium >10% of household income needs explanation. Tax returns required over $1,000,000 face. Non-working spouse: up to working spouse's face, max $500,000 without call (p8).
- Certificate backdating: up to 90 days to save age; never to make someone eligible for a product/rider (p6).
- Jet Term/Jet WL apps must be face-to-face (no Zoom/video); first premium drafted on approval (p4).
