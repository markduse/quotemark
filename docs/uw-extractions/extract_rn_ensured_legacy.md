## Product identification (p1, p20)

This IS the **Ensured Legacy Final Expense** agent training guide. Three products / four rate classes:

| Product | Form Series | Rate classes |
|---|---|---|
| Simplified Issue Whole Life (SIWL) | 211311 | SIWL **Preferred**, SIWL **Standard** (Tobacco/Non-Tobacco each) |
| Graded Death Benefit Whole Life (GDB) | 211312 | GDB (Tobacco/Non-Tobacco) |
| Guaranteed Issue Whole Life (GI) | 221309 | GI (**no tobacco class** — "If applying for the Guaranteed Issue plan, medical questions will be bypassed.") (p5) |

Maps directly to the app's Preferred / Standard / Graded / GI tiers.

## Issue ages & face amounts (p4)

Issue ages (age last birthday):
- **SIWL:** ages **50–85 (Standard)** and **50–75 (Preferred)**
- **GDB:** ages **50–85**
- **GI:** ages **50–80**

Face amounts:
- **SIWL:** **$5,000–$40,000 (Standard)** and **$25,000–$40,000 (Preferred)**
- **GDB:** **$5,000–$20,000**
- **GI:** **$3,000–$10,000**

(Confirmed again p10: "Maximum SIWL with Royal Neighbors is $40,000 and $20,000 for GDB, $10,000 for GI." Also p10: height/weight "only required for preferred rating. Note: Min face is $25,000 and ages 50–75.")

## Death benefit structure (p5)

- **SIWL:** Level death benefit, guaranteed level premium, whole life maturing at age 121.
- **GDB:** Death benefit is **30% of face in first certificate year, 70% in the second certificate year, and 100% thereafter**. 100% of face if death is accidental within the first two years.
- **GI:** Limited Graded Benefit if death by natural causes — **110% of earned Premiums Paid** (excluding rider premiums) **during Certificate Years 1 and 2; 100% of face in Certificate Year 3 and thereafter**. 100% of face for accidental death within the first two years.

## Underwriting mechanics (p5, p9–13)

- Health/medical requirements: "Yes or no, with reflexive medical questions when necessary. No paramed required." (p5)
- Instant decisions via e-App (24/7, not in CT) and phone interview (866-281-9228; M–F 8am–9pm CT, Sat 8am–3pm CT, closed Sunday); paper app = no immediate decision. (p9)
- Phone interview: interviewer obtains "voice signature authorization to order the **prescription authorization** and other information" then asks the medical questions; interviewer advises approve / decline / refer to Home Office. (p11)
- **Application health questions are NOT reproduced verbatim in this guide.** They live on Paper Application Form 231115 (with ICC/state versions), section 6 — the guide says "Review medical questions on the paper application under section 6 (Form 231115...), and Risk Assessment Guide on page 18." (p13)
- **Rx/med rules: no drug list is printed.** Only rule given: "If you have any questions regarding medications, or any of the items in the pre-qualification checklist above, please call Underwriting at (309) 788-4561, Option 1, Option 1, Option 1." (p13)

### Knockouts / not eligible for ANY product (SIWL, GDB, or GI) (p13)

- Anyone without a Social Security number
- Anyone who is not a U.S. citizen or Permanent Resident (Green Card holder)
- Anyone mentally incompetent or otherwise unable to make a valid contract
- Insured and/or Owners with a **felony** — not accepted regardless of face amount
- Anyone who already has $40,000 SIWL, $20,000 GDB, or $10,000 GI with Royal Neighbors
- Anyone who has three or more lapsed certificates with Royal Neighbors

## Riders (p6–8) — quick reference

- **Accelerated Death Benefit – Terminal Condition & Permanent Confinement** (221203): terminal = 12-month life expectancy; confinement = qualified nursing home ≥90 days prior to request, expected until death. Max accel = lesser of 75% of DB or $100,000; min $5,000; max admin fee $100. Face must be ≥$7,000. One acceleration per insured. No premium (lien w/ interest). **Available SIWL & GDB; NOT on GI.** Allowed in all states RNA does business (p14 FAQ).
- **Accidental Death Benefit Rider** (222140): extra premium; issue ages 50–70, expires at anniversary on/after age 80; face SIWL $5k–$40k / GDB $5k–$20k / GI $3k–$10k; always standard rating.
- **Charitable Giving Rider** (221113): no premium; lesser of 1% of face or $1,000 to charity; issue ages 50–85; all products.
- **Grandchild Rider** (221081): $60/yr flat, $5,000 coverage, all eligible grandchildren (90 days–17, or to 25 if enrolled in accredited education); issue ages 50–85; convertible.

## Admin details worth quoting (p4, p9, p13)

- Certificate fee: **$50/year (commissionable)**. Free look 30 days. Premiums payable to age 121.
- Payment: monthly/quarterly/annual EFT; direct bill quarterly/annual (paper). **First payment cannot be direct express, debit or credit card**; subsequent payments can (p14).
- e-App and phone app NOT available in Connecticut (paper only) (p9).
- Phone app: Owner and Payor must be the Insured; e-App/paper allow different owner/payor (p9).
- One re-date per certificate allowed within first 60 days of issue (p13).
- Reinstatement: within 3 years after grace period expires with evidence of insurability; 6% interest on past-due premiums (p6).
- Underwriting contact: (309) 788-4561, Option 1, Option 1, Option 1; UW_mailbox@royalneighbors.org (p13, p19).
