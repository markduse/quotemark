## 1) ROYAL NEIGHBORS OF AMERICA — Jet Term / Jet Whole Life / Jet SPWL
**Source:** `Royal Neighbors - Whole Life product - UW guidelines.pdf` (2980-B Rev. 6-2025, 32 pp, "Jet Life Underwriting Guidelines for Agents"). NOTE cover: "For SIWL and GDB, see Agent Guide 2996-B" — this guide covers the Jet (accelerated/fully-underwritten) products, NOT the final-expense SIWL/graded product.

**Structure:** Accelerated UW (point-of-sale) for Jet Term (15/20/30 yr), Jet WL (20-pay & Lifetime), Jet Juvenile WL, Jet SPWL (p.14). Jet Term/WL: ages 18-50 up to $500K, 51-60 up to $250K accelerated; otherwise Traditional UW (p.16, p.23). Traditional risk classes: Super Preferred NT, Preferred NT, Preferred Tobacco, Standard NT, Standard Tobacco, Tables 2–16 (p.23). This guide expresses outcomes as **Substandard / Decline / referral**, not Level-vs-Graded.

### A) COPD / Emphysema / Chronic bronchitis (p.15)
Under "Health conditions that could cause referral or rating for Accelerated Underwriting" — verbatim: "The following conditions may result in a decline or Substandard certificate":
- **Chronic bronchitis¹** — referral/rating
- **Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD)** — referral/rating (no footnote: eligible for consideration on all Jet products)
- **Emphysema¹** — referral/rating
- Footnote ¹ (p.15, verbatim): "**Only available for consideration for Jet SPWL and Traditional Underwriting.**" → i.e., footnoted conditions are knocked out of accelerated Jet Term/Jet WL and can only be considered via SPWL or Traditional (paramed) path.
- No oxygen/inhaler-specific rules printed anywhere in this guide.

**Tobacco (p.11):** Tobacco user if ANY of the following in the **past 12 months**: any tobacco/nicotine delivery system; chewing tobacco or snuff; cigarettes; cigars regardless of frequency; pipe; eCigarette/vaping; nicotine replacement therapy (gum, patch, etc.). Rate reclassification available after **stopped tobacco one year**. Preferred classes (p.24, fully underwritten only): Super Preferred = "No tobacco use in past five years"; Preferred = "No tobacco use in past three years"; Preferred Tobacco = "Current use, or use within past three years"; Standard NT/T = "No tobacco use within last 12 months/current use".

### B) Heart / Cardiovascular (p.15)
Referral-or-rating list (may = decline or Substandard); ¹ = Jet SPWL/Traditional only:
- Abnormal Heart Rhythm¹ · Atrial fibrillation¹ · Blood clot¹ · Clotting disorder¹ · Coronary artery disease¹ · Deep vein thrombosis (DVT)¹ · Heart attack¹ · Heart valve disease¹ · Pacemaker¹ · Pulmonary embolism¹ · **Stent, angioplasty or bypass¹** · **Transient ischemic attack (TIA)** (no footnote)

**Declined Health Conditions (p.15)** — auto-decline all Jet products:
- **Aneurysm** · **Cardiomyopathy** · **Cerebral hemorrhage** · **Congestive heart failure** · **Defibrillator**
- Also (context): ALS, Alzheimer's, Bipolar disorder, Cirrhosis, Cognitive impairment, Currently hospitalized, Currently residing in assisted living/nursing home, Cystic Fibrosis, Dementia, HIV/AIDS.
- Global note (p.15, verbatim): "NOTE: If your client has been advised by a member of the medical profession to have any diagnostic test, hospitalization, or surgery that has not been completed, the case will be declined."

**Conditional Receipt (p.8):** not available "if within the past 12 months the Proposed Insured has been treated for or had any known heart trouble, stroke, or cancer" (also if face > $1M).

**Preferred family-history windows (p.24):** Super Preferred = "No incidence of coronary or cardiovascular disease or cancer in either parent or sibling prior to age 60"; Preferred & Preferred Tobacco = "No death from coronary or cardiovascular disease, or cancer in either parent or siblings prior to age 60". Cholesterol/BP gates also on p.24 (SP: chol ≤220 & ratio ≤5.0, BP ≤130/80 no treatment; Pref/Pref Tob: chol ≤240, ratio ≤6.0, BP ≤135/85 controlled; Std: chol 241–299, ratio 6.1–8.5, BP ≤150/90 controlled).

### C) Cancer (p.15)
- **Melanoma¹** — referral/rating; SPWL/Traditional consideration only (footnote ¹).
- **Declined:** "Cancer (other than basal cell skin cancer)" · "Currently undergoing chemotherapy or radiation treatment for cancer" · "Currently undergoing (or recommended to have) testing or further evaluation for a condition that has not been diagnosed".
- No remission-window rules printed (no "2 yr/5 yr" language in this guide) — everything routes through referral/risk-assessment email (UW.mailbox@royalneighbors.org, p.10).

### Supplemental — RNA final-expense SIWL/GDB (NOT in this PDF; from `Cheatsheet.pdf` p.1, "RNA" row; unofficial IMO cheat sheet)
Issue 50-85, max $25K, phone app, PHI yes: COPD "**Grade - No O2**" · AFIB **Level** · CHF **No** (decline) · Heart Surgery **> 2 yrs** · Angina **Graded** · TIA/Stroke **Level > 2 Yrs** · **Cancer Level > 2 Yrs** · Insulin Level · Wheelchair No · Dialysis No. (Confirm against Agent Guide 2996-B — not in folder.)

## 2) KSKJ LIFE — Precision Plan / Precision Plan Plus (Simplified-Issue Whole Life)
**Source:** `KSKJ underwriting guide.pdf` (PPPUG2025.04 "Precision Plan Plus Field Underwriting Guide", 12 pp). Decision engine: e-App + Rx history + MVR + criminal background (p.4); classes **Standard to Table 16 substandard** (p.2) — outcomes are Standard/Substandard bands or auto-decline (no graded plan discussed). e-App decision is final offer (p.2). Conditions with prescribed meds MUST be disclosed via reflexive questions (p.3).

### A) COPD / Emphysema / Chronic bronchitis
**Automatic Declines (p.5)** — "Applications should not be written on individuals with the following conditions":
- "**COPD/Emphysema and on oxygen**" → DECLINE
- "**COPD/Emphysema and currently smoking**" → DECLINE
- "**Emphysema**" → listed independently as an automatic decline (p.5)
- Related respiratory declines: Cystic Fibrosis · Pulmonary Fibrosis · Pulmonary Hypertension · Lung Cancer · Mesothelioma
**Probable ratings (p.12)** (assume no co-morbids/build/driving issues):
- "Respiratory conditions — **COPD: mid to high substandard**; **Asthma, Bronchitis: low to mid substandard**"
- Co-morbid flags that impact rating (p.12): "**Smoking and respiratory condition**"; "Heart Disease with any of the following: Diabetes, **COPD**, Smoking, Obesity"
**Meds (Knockout Medications, pp.6-11):** Nintedanib (Idiopathic Pulmonary Fibrosis) · Pirfenidone (IPF) · Beractant/Calfactant/Curosurf/Infasurf/Poractant alfa/Survanta (ARDS) · Dornase Alfa/Elexacaftor/Ivacaftor/Lumacaftor/Tezacaftor (Cystic Fibrosis). Lists "not intended to be exhaustive."

### B) Heart / Cardiovascular
**Automatic Declines (p.5):**
- "Congestive Heart Failure (chronic)" · "Cardiomyopathy" (via meds; CHF/cardiomyopathy also in medical-factors list p.4)
- "**CVA-Stroke (3 or more strokes or 2 within a 1-year time frame)**"
- "**Multiple strokes or heart attacks**"
- "Aneurysm (unoperated)"
- "Double valve repair"
- "**Valve replacement in the last year**"
- "Blood Pressure exceeding 180/100"
- "Unstable conditions such as: **Angina, A-fib, Hypertension, Deep Vein Thrombosis**"
- "Peripheral Artery Disease"
**Probable ratings (p.12):**
- "**Mild heart attack more than 3 years ago: low substandard**"
- "**Heart attack within the last 3 years: high substandard**"
- "Heart defects — **A-fib or Tachycardia: low to mid substandard**"
- "Heart disorders — **Coronary Artery Disease or Cardiomyopathy: low to mid substandard**"
- "**Stroke: low to high substandard**"
- "Vascular conditions — **Angioplasty, blood clots, deep vein thrombosis, stent, or pacemaker: mid to high substandard**"
- Co-morbid flag: "Heart Disease with any of: Diabetes, COPD, Smoking, Obesity"
**Medical factors reviewed (p.4):** Aneurysm; Arrhythmia (significant); Cardiomyopathy; Cardiac Failure; Cerebrovascular Disease; Congenital Heart Disease; Coronary Heart Disease; Heart Murmur (Significant) or Irregular Heart Rhythm; Hypertension requiring 2 or more medications; Coagulation Disorder.
**Knockout meds (pp.6-11):** **Entresto** (Heart Failure) · **Sacubitril-Valsartan** (all forms; Heart Failure) · **Isosorbide dinitrate and Hydralazine (BiDil)** (CHF) · **Ivabradine / Corlanor** (Cardiomyopathy / CHF) · **Digoxin** (Cardiomyopathy) · Dexrazoxane (Cardiomyopathy).

### C) Cancer
**Automatic Declines (p.5):**
- "**Cancer treatment in the last 2 years other than for Basal Cell Carcinoma**"
- "**Cancer (current treatment)**"
- Site-specific auto-declines regardless of timing: **Bile Duct Cancer · Brain Tumor · Brain Cancer · Esophageal Cancer · Hodgkin's Lymphoma · Kaposi's Sarcoma · Liver Cancer · Lung Cancer · Mesothelioma · Multiple Myeloma · Neuroblastoma · Pancreatic Cancer**
- "Bone Marrow Transplant" · "Organ transplant" · "Waiting test results" · "Pending surgery or procedure"
**Risk factor (p.4):** "Cancer or tumor — excluding Basal Cell" is a rated medical factor (i.e., basal cell is a non-factor).
**Co-morbid flag (p.12):** "Autoimmune Disease and Cancer".
**Knockout meds (pp.6-11, cancer-flagged; partial):** Afatinib/Alectinib/Amivantamab/Capmatinib/Cemiplimab/Ceritinib/Dacomitinib/Durvalumab/Erlotinib/Gefitinib/Lorlatinib/Mobocertinib/Necitumumab/Osimertinib/Porfimer/Selpercatinib/Sotorasib/Tepotinib/Vinorelbine (Lung/NSCLC) · Alkeran/Carfilzomib/Daratumumab/Elotuzumab/Evomela/Isatuximab/Ixazomib/Melphalan/Pomalidomide/Thalidomide (Multiple Myeloma) · Ipilimumab/Aldesleukin/Interferon Alfa-2b (Melanoma etc.) · Degarelix/Firmagon (Prostate) · Balversa/Erdafitinib (Bladder) · Ixabepilone/Olaparib (Breast/Ovarian) · Camptosar/Irinotecan/Streptozocin/Sunitinib (Pancreatic/Colon/Rectal) · Xgeva/Zoledronic Acid (Bone) · Infigratinib/Pemigatinib (Cholangiocarcinoma/Liver/Bile Duct) · Lomustine/Danyelza/Dinutuximab/Naxitamab/Unituxin (Brain tumor/Neuroblastoma) · Vincristine (Hodgkin's) etc. "Not intended to be exhaustive."

## 3) LIFESHIELD NATIONAL — The Survivor (Level / Graded Death Benefit)
**Source:** `Survivor Agent Underwriting Guide V6.pdf` (08/2023, **3 pages total**). Issue ages 45-80; DB $5,000–$30,000; individual or Joint First-to-Die (p.2).

**Tier logic — verbatim (p.2):**
- "All **'No' answers to Section A and Sections B** will result in a **Level Benefit plan** of either tobacco or non-tobacco."
- "Any **'Yes' response to questions in Sections A is not an acceptable risk** and should not be submitted."  → Section A = knockout/decline.
- "Any **'Yes' response in Sections B** will result in a **Graded Death Benefit plan** with no change in premium rate (or commission)."

**IMPORTANT GAP:** This guide does **not reproduce the Section A / Section B question text**. There are NO printed condition-specific rules (no COPD, heart, or cancer windows) anywhere in the 3-page PDF. The A/B questions live on the application itself, which is not in the folder. So per-condition COPD/heart/cancer mapping for Survivor **cannot be extracted from this PDF** — flag for Mark to supply the Survivor application (Sections A & B) if the quoting app needs condition-level routing for LifeShield.

**What the guide does specify:**
- Home Office processing (p.2): "may include an inquiry to MIB, Inc or a Prescription Drug Report from Milliman IntelliScript. If the application is incomplete or inconsistent with MIB or Milliman, a phone interview will be conducted with the applicant."
- In-person only: "(No Phone or Virtual Sales Permitted)" (p.2); ID verification required.
- Automatic declines OTHER than poor health (p.3): app taken by phone/virtually; personal info incomplete and not furnished on request; "application was not received in the home office **within 10 days** of the application being signed"; applicant doesn't recall/denies signing; unable/unwilling to complete phone interview.
- Tobacco (p.3): "For Joint applications, the premium rate is based on the age of the oldest applicant and whether either uses **tobacco (including marijuana, vapes, or e-cigs)**." (Tobacco affects rate class Level Tobacco vs Non-Tobacco, not Graded placement.)
- List all doctors/hospitals visited within last 24 months (p.3).

## 4) ACCENDO INSURANCE CO. (CVS Health / Aetna affiliate) — Final Expense WL ("Protection Series")
**Source A:** `Accendo-Brochure.pdf` (ACCFE05984, ©2020, 8 pp) — **consumer brochure; contains NO underwriting/health rules.** Product facts only (p.5):
- **Level Plans:** full face from issue date, accidental or natural. Issue/face: 40-55 $2,000–$50,000 · 56-65 $2,000–$40,000 · 66-75 $2,000–$30,000 · 76-89 $2,000–$25,000. $40 annual policy fee. Riders (Level only): Accelerated Death Benefit (up to 50%, $1,000–$15,000, $200 fee, 12-mo life expectancy); Accidental Death (100% of face, issue 40-70); Children's Term ($2,500 units, max $10,000/child, 9 children max).
- **Modified Plan:** issue 40-75, $2,000–$25,000. "Non-accidental death: Policy years 1 and 2 = **110% of earned premium**; Policy year 3 = full benefit." Accidental death = full benefit immediately.

**Source B (fills the UW gap; same folder):** `Final-Expense-Underwriting-Cheat-Sheet-1.pdf` — Family First Life "Final Expense Grid Sheet", ACCENDO column (grid pp.1-8) + CVS/Aetna overview page (p.10). Unofficial IMO grid — verify against carrier app.
**Tier structure (p.10, verbatim):** Issue ages 40-89, $2,000–$50,000 depending on age, "**NO HEIGHT AND WEIGHT CHARTS**".
- "PREFERRED LEVEL COVERAGE — Immediate coverage with answering NO to all questions on Application"
- "STANDARD LEVEL COVERAGE — Still immediate coverage; answered one questions in Part C"
- "MODIFIED COVERAGE — Return of Premiums if death occurs within the first two years"

### A) COPD / respiratory (grid pp.2-3, 6-7)
- **COPD → Standard** (p.3)
- **Emphysema (chronic) → Standard** (p.4)
- **Bronchitis (chronic) → Standard** (p.2)
- **Asthma (chronic) → Standard** (p.2)
- **Oxygen: "Within 1 year - DECLINE (CPAP OK)"** (p.6)
- **Pulmonary fibrosis → DECLINE** (p.7)
- Sleep apnea: Not asked (p.8). No inhaler-specific rule in grid. Tobacco affects rate class only (not asked in grid).

### B) Heart / cardiovascular (grid pp.1-2, 4-8)
Signature Accendo 3-step window (Modified → Standard → Preferred):
- **Heart attack: "Within 1 year - Modified; Between 1-2 years - Standard; > 2 yrs - Preferred"** (p.5)
- **Heart surgery:** same windows (p.5) — stents & angioplasty route here ("Stent: Not asked - See Heart Surgery" p.8; "Angioplasty: See Heart Surgery" p.1)
- **Heart valve replacement:** same windows (p.5)
- **Pacemaker / defibrillator implant: Within 1 year - Modified; Between 1-2 years - Standard; > 2 yrs - Preferred** (p.7)
- **Angina: "Treated Within 1 year - Modified; Between 1-2 years - Standard; > 2 yrs - Preferred"** (p.1)
- **Stroke / TIA: "Within 1 year - Modified; Between 1-2 years - Standard; > 2 yrs - Preferred"** (p.8)
- **Aneurysm: "Within 1 Year - Modified"** (p.1)
- **CHF / diastolic heart failure → DECLINE** (p.4)
- **Coronary artery disease: See Heart Disease** (heart-attack/surgery windows) (p.4)
- **AFib / irregular heartbeat: Not asked - Allowed** (pp.1, 5)
- Blood clots: "Not asked - Ask clients questions about Stents or Heart Surgery" (p.2)

### C) Cancer (grid pp.2, 6)
- **Cancer (other than basal cell): "Current OR treated within 2 years OR recurring - DECLINE; Basal cell and Squamous - Preferred; > 2 yrs - Preferred"** (p.2)
- **Melanoma: "Within 3 years - Modified (but excludes basal / squamous cell skin cancer)"** (p.6) — i.e., melanoma >3 yrs clean; basal/squamous never counted.
- **Bone marrow transplant → DECLINE** (p.2) · **Organ transplant → DECLINE** (p.8) · **Terminal illness → DECLINE** (p.8)

### Other notable Accendo knockouts (context, grid): ALS, Alzheimer's/dementia, cerebral palsy, cystic fibrosis, Huntington's, sickle cell anemia, wheelchair/scooter use, amputation due to disease/diabetes → DECLINE; dialysis within 1 year → DECLINE; alcohol/drug treatment within 2 years → Modified; DUI within 2 years → Modified; cirrhosis treated within 2 years → Modified; kidney disease → Graded[sic]/Modified tier; diabetic complications (coma, insulin shock, neuropathy, nephropathy, retinopathy) → Modified, amputation → DECLINE.

## Cross-carrier gaps & flags for the quoting app
1. **LifeShield Survivor:** condition-level rules are NOT in the V6 guide — need the actual application (Sections A/B) to encode COPD/heart/cancer routing. Guide only defines A=decline, B=Graded, all-No=Level.
2. **Accendo:** the brochure in the folder is consumer-facing with zero UW content; all condition rules above come from the FFL grid sheet (unofficial). Verify windows against the Accendo app Part B/C before shipping.
3. **Royal Neighbors:** this PDF is the JET (underwritten) guide. RNA's final-expense SIWL/GDB rules live in Agent Guide 2996-B (not in folder); only the unofficial `Cheatsheet.pdf` RNA row covers it.
4. **KSKJ:** outcomes are Standard→Table 16 or decline (no graded tier); e-App decision is final. Non-disclosure of medicated conditions can worsen the offer (p.2-3).
5. All four sources carry "list is not exhaustive / final decisions may vary" disclaimers.
