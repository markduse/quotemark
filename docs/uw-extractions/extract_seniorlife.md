# A) COPD / EMPHYSEMA / CHRONIC BRONCHITIS / RESPIRATORY

### Respiratory Conditions (High-Risk) — combined guide p.11; FU guide p.7; SI guide p.6
Condition list (verbatim): *"Acute Respiratory Distress Syndrome (ARDS), Asbestosis, Bronchiectasis, Chronic Obstruction Airway Disease, Chronic Obstructive Disease, Chronic Obstructive Respiratory Disease, Emphysema Obstructive Lung Disease, Pneumoconiosis"*
- **FU: NOT eligible** ("Not eligible for our Fully Underwritten products" — FU guide p.7)
- **SI: NOT eligible**
- **Graded: ONLY option** — verbatim: "Only Eligible for our Graded Benefit Product" (p.11 combined; p.6 SI guide)
- No severity/inhaler gradation given — ANY COPD/emphysema diagnosis → Graded, period.

### Oxygen — Automatic-denial list (combined guide pp.13–14; Graded guide pp.2–3)
- **"Oxygen Therapy"** appears on the auto-denial list: "Automatic Denials for Simplified Issue/Fully Underwritten products. The insured is only eligible for our Graded Product." (p.13)
  - NOTE: oxygen does NOT knock out Graded — Graded remains available unless a Graded knockout question (below) hits.
- Related respiratory auto-denials (SI/FU denied → Graded only): Alpha-1 antitrypsin (AAT), Black Lung Disease (CWP), Cor Pulmonale, Cystic Fibrosis, Idiopathic Pulmonary Fibrosis, Lung Cancer, Lung Transplant, Pulmonary Hypertension, Silicosis, Dependence on Aspirator, Dependence on Respirator (pp.13–14).

### Inhalers / tobacco with COPD
- No inhaler-specific or tobacco-with-COPD rules appear in any of these documents.
- Only tobacco-adjacent rule: Drug Use – Low Risk (marijuana/THC/CBD, p.9): SI accepts current use or use within last 12 months (≤3x/week, or 4–5x/week) but "Tobacco Rates will be used"; >5x/week → Graded. FU accepts "Last used over 2 years ago" or "Last used within the past within the 2 years" (sic).

### Graded knockout questions (verbatim, combined guide p.13; Graded guide p.2)
"lf the insured answers 'Yes' to any of the 2 questions listed below, then the insured does not qualify for our Graded Benefit Product.
1: Are you currently confined to a bed, or in a hospital, clinic, rest or convalescent home?
2: Have you ever been diagnosed by a member of the medical profession or tested positive for Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (AIDS) or Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV). This includes having filled any prescriptions for Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (AIDS) or Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV)."

# B) HEART / CARDIOVASCULAR

### Heart Attack (Cardiac Arrest, Heart Attack, Myocardial Infarction) — combined p.10; FU p.6; SI p.5
- **FU AND SI eligible** (same section covers both):
  - "I have had only 1 heart attack, which was more than 3 years ago. My latest test results showed good blood flow (minimal blockage or an Ejection Fraction above 55%)."
  - "I have had only 1 heart attack, which was more than 2 years ago. My latest test results showed average blood flow (moderate blockage or an Ejection Fraction between 45% and 54%)."
- **Graded**:
  - "I have had only 1 heart attack, which was between 1 and 2 years ago. My latest test results showed good blood flow (minimal blockage or an Ejection Fraction between 35% and 44%)." (sic — guide's own wording)
  - "I have had multiple heart attacks or my latest test results showed fair or poor blood flow (significant blockage or an Ejection Fraction less than 44%)."
  - "I have had only 1 heart attack, which was in the last year. My latest test results showed average blood flow (moderate blockage or an Ejection Fraction between 45% and 54%)."
- Implication: MI within 1 yr with EF <45%, or any scenario worse than above → not listed; auto-denial list governs (Heart Failure/CHF etc. deny SI/FU; Graded still possible unless knockout).

### Heart Defects — combined p.10; FU p.6; SI p.5 — **includes AFIB and pacemaker logic**
Condition list (verbatim): *"Adams-Stokes Syndrome, Asymmetric Septal Hypertrophy, Atrial Fibrillation, Atrial Flutter, Atrial Septal Defect, Atrial Tachycardia, Atrioventricular Block, Balloon Mitral Valve, Barlow's Syndrome, Bicuspid Aortic Valve, Bigeminy, Billowing Mitral Valve Syndrome, Bundle Brach Blocks, Carotid Sinus Syndrome, Click-Murmur Syndrome, Floppy-Valve Syndrome, Mitral Valve Prolapse, Myxomatous Mitral Valve Disease, Premature Ventricular Contractions, Premature Ventricular Complex, Ventricular Fibrillation"*
- **FU AND SI eligible**:
  - "Well-controlled with minimal symptoms, no need for a pacemaker/implant and does not need to be repaired with surgery."
  - "My condition is repaired. I have recovered, am stable with no heart complications and no need for additional surgery." (an installed/completed pacemaker or repair that is stable fits here)
- **Graded**:
  - "My condition is repaired. I occasionally experience symptoms but have no additional heart complications and no need for additional surgery."
  - "My condition is repaired. I frequently experience symptoms or have additional heart complications, but no need for additional surgery."
  - "My doctor has recommended surgery or a pacemaker/implant, which has not been completed." ← pending pacemaker/surgery = Graded

### Heart Disease (CAD / valves / carotid) — combined p.10; FU p.6; SI p.5
Condition list (verbatim): *"Aortic Valve Regurgitation, Aortic Valve Stenosis, Carotid Artery Disease, Carotid Stenosis, Congenital Heart Conditions, Congenital Heart Disease, Coronary Artry Disease, Coronary Heart Disease, Left Ventricular Dysfunction"*
- **FU**: "I have good blood flow (minimal blockage or an Ejection Fraction above 55%), and no history of heart attacks."
- **SI**: "I have average blood flow (moderate blockage or an Ejection Fraction between 45% and 54%), and no history of heart attacks."
- **Graded** (combined guide p.10):
  - "I have fair blood flow (significant blockage or an Ejection Fraction between 35% and 44%), and no history of heart attacks."
  - "I've had only 1 heart attack and that was over 2 years ago."
  - "I had a heart attack within the past 2 years, and/or have had multiple heart attacks."
- **DISCREPANCY:** SI-only guide p.5 lists "I had a heart attack within the past 2 years, and/or have had multiple heart attacks." under the SI heading — the combined guide (p.10) puts it under Graded. Treat combined guide as authoritative (recent/multiple MI + heart disease = Graded); flag to carrier if it matters.

### Auto-denial cardiac list (SI/FU denied → Graded only) — combined pp.13–14; Graded guide pp.2–3
Cardiomyopathy; Congestive Heart Failure; Heart Failure; Heart Replacement; Hypertrophic Obstructive Cardiomyopathy (HOCM); Idiopathic Hypertrophic Sub-Aortic Stenosis (IHSS); Double Valve Repair; Double Valve Replacement; Cor Pulmonale; Pulmonary Hypertension; Critical Limb-Threatening Ischemia (CLTI); Dependence on Artificial Heart.
- **So: CHF and cardiomyopathy = automatic SI/FU denial; Graded only** (subject to the 2 knockout questions).
- Single valve repair/replacement is NOT on the denial list (only "Double") — would fall under Heart Defects/Heart Disease grids above.

### Angina, stents, bypass, aneurysm
- **Not explicitly named anywhere** in these guides. Nearest mappings: angina/stent/bypass → "Coronary Artery Disease/Coronary Heart Disease" blockage-EF grid above; per p.2 note, unlisted conditions are "subject to underwriting approval."
- Aneurysm: not listed (aortic aneurysm absent). Amaurosis Fugax/blood clots fall in Vascular Group C.

### Stroke / TIA — combined p.12; FU p.7; SI p.6
Condition list (verbatim): *"Cerebral Ischemia, Cerebrovascular Accident, Hemorrhagic Stroke, Ischemic Stroke, Lacunar Infarct, Mini Stroke, Stroke, Transient Cerebral Ischemia, Transient Ischemic Attack"*
- **FU: NOT eligible** ("Not eligible for our Fully Underwritten products" — FU p.7). Any stroke/TIA ever kills FU.
- **SI**: "I have had only 1 stroke which I recovered from without complications over 2 years ago."
- **Graded**:
  - "I have had only 1 stroke which I recovered from without complication between 1 and 2 years ago."
  - "I have had only 1 stroke which I recovered from without complications within the past year."
  - "I have had more than 1 stroke, which I recovered from without related complications."
  - "I am still recovering from a stroke / experiencing complication."

### Hypertension — combined p.9; FU p.5; SI p.4
- Controlled BP: "Eligible for both our Fully Underwritten and Simplified Issue products."
- Uncontrolled ("would be eligible for our Graded Product"): "BP 160/100 (High)"; "BP at 90/40 (Low)"; "Non-Compliant with Medications"; "ER Visits due to blood pressure"; "Hospital Stays due to blood pressure".

### Vascular Conditions (FU: NONE — FU guide p.7: "Vascular Conditions are Not eligible for our Fully Underwritten products")
**Group A** (Carotid Bruits, Renal Artery Disease, Renal Artery Stenosis, Renal Vascular Disease, Renovascular Hypertension, Subclavian Steal Syndrome) — combined p.12; SI p.7:
- SI: "Mild condition (grade I), with normal heart and kidney function and well controlled cholesterol and blood pressure (about 155/80)."
- Graded: grade II (~160/85); grade III abnormal heart/kidney function (~165/90); "I don't know the severity of my condition or I have poorly controlled blood-pressure (about 170/100 or above)"; "I have end-stage renal failure or congestive heart failure or, have been advised by my doctor to get surgery."

**Group B** (Arteriosclerosis, Arteriosclerosis Obliterans, Atherosclerosis, Claudication, Hardening of the Arteries, Peripheral Artery Disease (PAD), Peripheral Vascular Disease (PV)) — combined p.12; SI p.7:
- SI: "I have no blockage or, blockage less than 30% that is being treated with diet, exercise and medication."
- Graded: blockage <50% treated; <60% treated; "severe condition with blockage more than 60%"; "I'm unsure of the severity of my condition or the amount of blockage I have."

**Group C** (Amaurosis Fugax, Blood Clots, Cockett's Syndrome, Deep Vein Thrombosis (DVT), Extrinsic Venous Compression, Iliac Vein Compression Syndrome, Iliocaval Venous Compression Syndrome, May-Thurner Syndrome (MTS), Moyamoya Disease, Transient Visual Loss (TVL), Varicose Veins, Varicosis, Vasculitis, Venous Spur) — combined p.12; SI p.7:
- SI: "My condition has been stable for over 3 years ago. I have no permanent organ damage and do not require surgery." / "stable for 2 to 3 years..."
- Graded: stable 1–2 yrs; stable <1 yr; "My condition is unstable or, I have permanent organ damage, or require surgery."

# FILE 5 — ELCO Mutual "Silver Eagle" (SEB-09-22) — NO UNDERWRITING RULES
Consumer brochure only; contains zero condition-level rules, no knockout questions. Product structure (p.3):
- **Immediate coverage classes: Premier, Plus, Standard** — issue ages 50–85, face $2,500–$35,000, immediate full death benefit, participating.
- **Graded** — ages 50–80, $2,500–$25,000: "30% of the face amount in year one and 70% in year two. The full benefit becomes available at the beginning of the third policy year."
- **Modified** — ages 50–80, $2,500–$25,000: Return of Premium + Interest in first two policy years; full benefit at start of year 3; participating.
- **Guaranteed Issue (GI)** — ages 50–80, $2,500–$25,000: Return of Premium + Interest first two years; NOT participating.
- Single Pay Life Rider: Premier/Plus/Standard only, ages 50–80, $1,000–$35,000, immediate, no policy fee.
- Qualification (p.2, verbatim): "Just answer a few basic health questions and complete a short telephone interview. Decisions are made instantly, and no medical exams or tests are ever required."
- Policy forms: ICC21-FEAPP21, ICC21 GBWL21, ICC21 GIWL 21. To get ELCO's actual health-question grid you need the FEAPP21 application or ELCO's agent UW guide — not in this folder.
