## 2. UW process (pp.4, 8)

- Digital app (iGO e-App/iPipeline), no exams or labs, "no-touch underwriter review," real-time decision within minutes; referrals to underwriter limited mainly to nonmedical risks (insurable interest, total coverage line, immigration status) (p.4).
- **Electronic medical data:** "diagnostic and prescription data direct from the healthcare provider" replaces long health questionnaires; ordered through Transamerica-approved vendors. Health-data vendor = **Milliman** (client dispute contact: FCRAReport@milliman.com, 877-211-4816) (p.8). **So Rx history is checked — meds drive decisions even if not disclosed** (see case studies §8).
- Application valid 90 days; cases close after 45 days if outstanding requirements (p.8).
- The verbatim application knockout questions are **NOT printed in this guide** — it references "an upfront eligibility question" plus "short, straightforward medical and lifestyle questions" (p.4) but does not reproduce them. Use the iGO app / TA Express doc for question text.
- "Not all applicants will qualify for nonmedical underwriting. It is not guaranteed issue and the underwriter reserves the right to request additional medical exams and data." (p.28)

## 3. General tier-combination rules — Adults 18+ (p.10)

Single-condition chart (§5) gives each condition's rating; final class comes from combining:

- **PREFERRED if:** medical conditions, lifestyle factors, and height/weight are ALL Preferred.
- **STANDARD if:** ALL medical conditions Preferred and ALL lifestyle + H/W Standard, **OR** one medical condition (rated Standard), H/W Preferred, and ALL lifestyle Preferred or Standard.
- **GRADED (Easy Solution) if:** one medical condition rated Graded, H/W Standard or Preferred, all lifestyle Graded or better; **OR two medical conditions that are Standard**, H/W Standard or Preferred, lifestyle Graded or better; **OR** all lifestyle + H/W Graded and all medical conditions (if any) Preferred.
- **DECLINED if:** any one condition or lifestyle factor rated Decline; **OR** H/W Decline (over Graded max weight); **OR four or more medical conditions** that are Standard or Graded.
- Implied by case study #3 (p.19): one Standard-rated condition + Standard (not Preferred) build = **Graded**.

## 4. Activity Credit (p.9) — adults 18+ only

Qualification: routine activity (walking the dog, gardening, mowing, manual labor, jogging, weights, etc.) **3+ days/week, ≥10 consecutive minutes each time**.
- Scenario 1: H/W Preferred + ONLY ONE of {respiratory disease/disorder such as COPD, black lung, or chronic bronchitis; Stroke or TIA; hospitalization within last 12 months} → rating improves **Standard → Preferred**.
- Scenario 2: H/W is the only risk factor (e.g., male 5'6" 250 lb, no conditions) + exercise → **Standard → Preferred**.

## 7. Riders (pp.5–7)

- **ADB Rider w/ Nursing Home Benefit** (auto-included where available; NOT CA/FL): requires continuous nursing-home confinement 90 days + physician certification of remaining until death; confinement from accident on/after rider effective date, or illness first manifesting >30 days after effective date. **Not available if applicant needs ADL assistance at application or within 2 years prior** (p.6).
- ADBR – Florida only: death reasonably expected within 12 months. TIR – California only: accelerate up to 100% of face less discounts/loans/admin/next-year premiums, death expected within 12 months (p.6).
- **Accidental Death Rider (ADR):** issue ages 18–70; death within 90 days of accidental injury; ADR amount must equal base face (pp.7, 27). Rates p.23 ($2.20–$5.46/unit by age).
- **Children's/Grandchildren's Rider (CGR):** $2.00/unit/child annual; parent issue ages 18–75; child ages 15 days–18; ≤9 children; face $1,000–min(base face, $5,000) per child; same face for all children; ineligible if terminal illness w/ death expected within 24 months; ≤$5,000 per child across all TA FE child riders; terminates at rider anniversary after child's 25th birthday; convertible after 2 years insured (ages 0–17 → juvenile standard; 18–25 → standard nontobacco) (p.7).
