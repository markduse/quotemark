All three InstaBrain products are instant-decision/accelerated UW, digital sales, e-signature, no underwriter touchpoints, no exams. Issue ages 18-60. Face $50K min; $1M max (limit of $1,000,000 across both InstaBrain and Pure Term products combined). Also universal knockouts on all three: **Medically declined in past 5 years = decline** (all classes NO), and **Pending surgery/tests/awaiting results = decline** ("Need results — MRI, CT Scan, Echocardiogram, Ultrasound, etc." — i.e., a pending echo/cardiac workup is an automatic no).

**Master lookback**: every "Conditions NOT Acceptable — Do NOT Submit" list is prefaced: *"within the last 10 years have you been diagnosed, tested, treated or consulted with or given medical advice for unless stated otherwise."* So each knockout below is a **10-year lookback** unless noted (e.g., illegal drug use in doc 1 is "Any History"). Corollary for cancer: a malignancy diagnosed/treated **more than 10 years ago** is outside the knockout question; within 10 years = automatic decline.

# DOC 1 — InstaBrain Term with Living Benefits, Full Guide IBT032025 (Mar 2025)

Risk classes (p2): Non-Tobacco: Preferred Plus / Preferred / Standard / Standard Extra. Tobacco: Preferred / Standard Extra. Terms 10/15/20 (ages 18-55 $1M; 56-60 $900K grading down $100K/yr to $500K at 60), 30-yr (NT 18-50, T 18-45, $1M).

## A) COPD / Respiratory — Doc 1
**Automatic DECLINES (p4, "Conditions NOT Acceptable at ALL — Do NOT Submit", 10-yr lookback):**
- **COPD including ARDS, Emphysema or Pulmonary Sarcoidosis** — decline. (Chronic bronchitis not named separately; it falls under COPD in practice — flag as decline in app logic.)
- **Ventilator Use** — decline.
- **Cystic Fibrosis** — decline.
**Acceptable (p5, Initial Quoting Criteria):**
- **Asthma (mild)**: YES at all 4 classes (Pref Plus / Pref / Std / Std Extra). Conditions: mild, ≤3 exacerbations (episodes) in past year, no shortness of breath climbing stairs, no tobacco, only occasional treatment.
- **COVID Virus**: YES all classes — no pending results, quarantine, symptoms, hospitalization, or residual effects for 30 days; **no history of ventilator use**.
- **Sleep Apnea** (p6): NO/NO/NO/**YES (Standard Extra only)** — sleep study must be completed and must use CPAP/BIPAP nightly; no current symptoms.

## C) Cancer — Doc 1
**Automatic DECLINES (p4, 10-yr lookback):**
- **Malignant Neoplasm/Cancer** — decline
- **Cancer/Melanoma, other than Basal/Squamous** — decline (melanoma explicitly a decline)
- **Leukemia, Lymphoma** — decline
- **Lynch Syndrome** — decline
- (Adjacent: Organ Transplant Recipient other than cornea — decline.)
**Acceptable skin cancers (p5):**
- **Cancer — Basal Cell**: YES all 4 classes — not currently undergoing treatment.
- **Cancer — Squamous Cell**: YES all 4 classes — **skin only**, not currently undergoing treatment.
**Remission window logic:** no graded remission table exists; the only "window" is the 10-year lookback on the knockout question. Any other cancer within 10 years = decline; basal/squamous skin = OK any class once treatment finished.
**Family history of cancer** (parent/sibling prior to 65): blocks Preferred Plus only (p5).

# DOC 4 — "fit-underwriting-flyer.pdf" = Mutual of Omaha Fit Underwriting Credit Program (1 page)

**Identification correction: this is NOT a Foresters piece.** It is United of Omaha Life Insurance Company (Mutual of Omaha), form 458577_0823 — the **Fit Underwriting Credit Program** for fully underwritten products: Income Advantage IUL, Life Protection Advantage IUL, **Term Life Answers**, AccumUL Answers. It is a table-credit program, not an instant-decision rule set. (Foresters' actual instant-decision UW rules are not in this file.)

Program eligibility (p1):
- Ages 18-75; min face $100,000; max face $5,000,000 (total inforce + applied with United of Omaha)
- **Nontobacco users only**
- Base rating after normal credits of **Table 4 or less**
- **Program EXCLUSIONS (cannot use Fit credits)**: "flat extra" ratings; current rateable substance abuse histories; **CAD prior to age 50**; **stroke**; **rateable cancers**; **Type 1 diabetes**; **HIV**
- Credit math: any **3** qualifying characteristics = 1 table credit; any **5** = 2 table credits; max 2 lifestyle characteristics counted. *Best final assessment via credits is Standard; Table 3 can only reduce to Table 1.*

Cardio/cancer-relevant credit characteristics (Medical list, p1):
- Negative cardiac testing within past 2 years: GXT, non-imaged or imaged (stress echo, perfusion study) echocardiogram, EBCT, or angiography
- GXT exercise performance >10 METS within past 2 years
- Optimal BP control (treated or untreated) averaging **135/85 or better**
- Cholesterol/HDL ratio <5.0; A1c <5.7
- Great family history — no parent/sibling deaths from any disease before age 70
- Build: Preferred or better build ages 18-60; Standard Plus or better build ages 61-75 (no numeric chart in this flyer)
- Ages 61-75 extras: serum albumin >4.2, BNP <100, normal CBC
Lifestyle list: preventative care/compliant follow-up within 12 months; no tobacco 10 years; income >$100K or net worth >$1M; preferred-or-better driving record.

**Quoting-app takeaway from Doc 4**: no decline/accept rules for COPD or specific cardiac events beyond the exclusions above — treat CAD-before-50, stroke, rateable cancer, T1D, HIV as "Fit credits unavailable" (still potentially insurable via normal full UW, just no credits).
