## Summary lists (pp. 11–13)
- **No plan:** AIDS/HIV, Alzheimer's, cognitive impairment, dementia, home health care, stroke w/ HHC or cognitive impairment, terminal illness, IV drug use.
- **CGWL only:** ADL assistance (no HHC), ALS, cancer-free <12 mo, cardiomyopathy, oxygen use, CHF, dialysis, organ transplant, stroke <12 mo, heart attack/heart surgery <12 mo, **hospitalized 2+ times in past 12 mo**, muscular dystrophy.
- **WL only (no term):** ALS, COPD, diabetes w/ complications, kidney disease, liver disease, Parkinson's, MS, sickle cell, heart attack/stroke 12 mo–2 yr.
- **WL + term:** bipolar, diabetes no complications, mild asthma, sleep apnea w/o oxygen, seizure history, heart attack/stroke >5 yr, TIA, Crohn's, rheumatoid arthritis, ulcerative colitis.

Not addressed in the guide: active chemo/radiation, pacemaker, non-diabetic neuropathy — Quotemarko keeps its prior rules for these.
